# Research evolution — Cedar Project 2

**Supplemental reflection, November 23, 2026.** Project 2 does not require a Research Evolution PDF or Edition A/B submission. This page traces documented questions, checks, and decisions; it does not claim I completed a separate pre-AI edition or an experiment that is not in the repository. ## The decision I was trying to make

Cedar Scholarship Foundation has a $20 million portfolio and pays $150,000 in scholarships each quarter. Its coursework objective is CPI plus 3% after costs over rolling five-year periods. I used the four funds carried forward from earlier coursework—BIL, IEF, LQD, and VT—to ask whether a committee should put **new capital** into a proposed portfolio process. I kept BIL as an *illustrative* stand-in for a short-term reserve. That is a modeling choice, not Cedar's approval to count fund shares as cash for scholarship payments. These choices and their origins are recorded in the [student decision log](student-decision-log.md) and [Lab 13 mandate](../recovered/Lab13/Lab13_Report.md).

## What I learned from the app and where I pushed back

| Question | Evidence I examined | What I concluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does a numerical pass justify investing? | The [saved baseline](../outputs/results.json) and [visible results](checkout-results.md) show a research candidate of **77.04% BIL / 22.96% VT**, with **5.50% estimated annual return** and **3.53% estimated annual volatility**. The solver was asked to clear a 5.50% illustrative hurdle and landed on it. | A pass under the model's assumptions is useful, but the estimate has almost no room for trading costs or weaker future VT returns. I said in my [committee presentation](transcript-video-3.txt) that I would not present this as a trade ticket. |
| Does the CPI number support the proposed reserve rule? | Codex proposed a historical CPI fallback. I reported checking three values in the stored vintage and calculating **336.298 / 324.245 − 1 ≈ 3.72%**; the [data card](signal-data-card.md) separates my reported check from the code's source checks. | The arithmetic supports the displayed reading. It does not show that raising the analytical BIL floor from 3% to 5% improves this baseline, because the candidate already holds 77.04% BIL. Cedar has not approved the rule. I would compare results with the rule turned off over the same dates before relying on it. |
| How dependent is the solution on the reserve requirement? | In my [locked changed-input record](changed-input-record.md), I predicted **before the run** that raising the *analytical* BIL floor from 3% to 85% would prevent the model from meeting the 5.50% hurdle. My 85% BIL / 15% VT check estimated about 4.84%. The [saved run](../outputs/locked_changed_input.json) found no feasible constrained portfolio. | The prediction and feasibility result agreed. Because no after-run portfolio existed, I could not compare after-run volatility with my prediction. This test did **not** change Cedar's real reserve policy. It showed the reserve–return trade-off that a committee must resolve. |
| Can I trust one set of optimized weights? | The [36-month stress](checkout-results.md) moved the mix to about **92.86% BIL / 7.14% VT**, compared with 77.04% / 22.96% at 60 months. | The allocation depends on the estimation window. As I explained in [Video 3](transcript-video-3.txt), I would test a reasonable range of windows rather than choose whichever output I like best. This stress does not establish future performance. |
| Could the committee implement the candidate? | The app marks the full mandate **Not Known** and deployment authorization **Fail**. Cedar's numerical risk limit, approval to treat BIL as reserve, current holdings, and actual trading costs are unresolved. Aidan's [README cold run](andrew-cold-run-by-aidan.md) reproduced the earlier baseline on his Mac, but he changed no input. | A working app and reproducible numbers do not establish client suitability. The missing risk limit is my first question: I cannot call 3.53% volatility acceptable without Cedar's approved boundary. Holdings and costs determine whether a trade fits turnover limits and clears the hurdle after implementation. |

## Where I landed

I recommend **rejecting deployment for now: 0% of new capital** to the proposed strategy, with Cedar's existing holdings left in place. I would keep the app and candidate for research. I would ask Cedar to confirm its risk limit, reserve treatment and scholarship payment plan, current holdings, and trading costs. Then I would rerun the model under approved inputs and check whether a small pilot remains within the limits and clears the hurdle after costs. If it does not, I would revise the research strategy rather than lower Cedar's objective merely to obtain a passing result. This is the decision I presented in the [replacement committee video](https://youtu.be/UfHrL-9ilAM).

The available evidence does not include Cedar's actual positions, a costed trade plan, or a same-date comparison of the CPI rule on and off. I therefore do not claim the app is ready to invest Cedar's money. The [validation and AI-use record](validation-and-ai-use.md) identifies the technical checks and the limits of what I personally verified.
